A photo made for each recipe.

Recipe photos are AI-generated illustrations. Opening a recipe without a photo requests an image based on its name, ingredients and cooking method. A completed image is saved and reused on later visits.

The illustration can differ from the meal you cook. Ingredient quantities and cooking instructions are given in the recipe itself.

A neutral placeholder appears until a generated photo is ready. If the image service is offline, already-saved images remain available.
